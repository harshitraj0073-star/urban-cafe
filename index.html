About Our Café

Fresh Ingredients, Honest Recipes.

Urban Café was started with a simple idea: to make a comfortable place where students, friends, and families can enjoy delicious food and real coffee at reasonable prices.

We source our coffee beans directly from south Indian coffee estates and bake our bread and brownies fresh every morning. Whether you need a peaceful spot to study or a fun place to catch up with friends, our doors are always open.
